Escape to Marbella Farms & Resort

Escape to Marbella Farms & Resort, a private luxury retreat near Moinabad (Kethireddypally), Hyderabad. Blending farmhouse warmth with upscale resort comfort, it is crafted for staycations, destination weddings, corporate retreats and intimate celebrations.

Villa • Luxury • 4BHK

Villa

A spacious 4BHK luxury villa crafted for comfort, style and large-group experiences.

  • Fully furnished interiors with two expansive living rooms
  • Dedicated dining area and convenient pantry
  • Comfortable for up to ~30 guests for group stays
Open-Air • Theatre • Lounge

Open-Air Theatre

An elevated outdoor entertainment lounge for immersive evenings under the open sky.

  • Smart theatre setup for movies, live matches and curated music
  • Cozy seating crafted for relaxed, late-evening gatherings
  • Sleek bar counter for seamless refreshments and conversations
Poolside • Lawn • Celebrations

Poolside Lawn

An elegant open-air lawn by the pool, perfect for day and evening celebrations.

  • Sundowner parties, receptions and live music-friendly setting
  • Easy flow between pool, lawn and villa for seamless hosting
  • Ideal for large gatherings, social events and curated experiences
Jamboree • Glass Hall • Events

Jamboree – Banquet Hall

A beautifully crafted glass banquet hall surrounded by lush greenery.

  • Transparent glass architecture with bright, scenic ambience
  • Perfect for weddings, receptions, sangeets and corporate events
  • Flexible décor and lighting with a natural indoor–outdoor feel
Utsav • Haldi • Mehendi

Utsav Lawn

A vibrant lawn curated especially for haldi, mehendi and pre-wedding rituals.

  • Warm, festive canvas for colour, flowers and traditional décor
  • Comfortable layouts for seating, photo corners and small stages
  • Close access to villa and other event zones for easy movement
